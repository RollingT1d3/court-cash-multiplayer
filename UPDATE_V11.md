# Court Cash Alpha v1.1 — Individual shot tendencies
Flexible offensive possessions weight the existing adhered-to strategic range distribution by scoring tendencies, with no voluntary sub-50 shots unless no stronger permitted option exists. Live By the 3 reserves its existing three-point share; Paint Mash reserves its paint share. Run & Gun and Old-School Buckets retain their specialist roles and reserve their role's primary range. Play Through Point and Run For The Hills retain their explicit action ranges. Fast breaks retain opportunity-based scoring but require 50+ skill for open options.

Dunk vs layup now compares both attributes using 0.50 + 0.012*(D-L) + 0.004*(D-80), clamped to 2–95%, with 0/100% exceptions when only one finish is rated 50+. Fadeaway selection retains existing pressure and IQ terms and applies the approved willingness multipliers/caps. Three-point distance gates are 80 for 30–35 ft, 85 for 36–40, 90 for 41–46, 95 for 47–62; rejected rolls revert to regulation-range attempts. Existing distance generation (23–30 or 36–62) remains intact; 31–35 is permitted for qualifying players but not newly generated. A new opposing-side possession within the final 3 seconds of the same quarter, excluding timeout starts, is the simulator's backcourt desperation proxy. The ordinary shot-clock threshold alone never bypasses gates.

Shot accuracy, assist rules, passing, player data, wheel odds, artwork, and all existing strategic shooter/role selection remain unchanged. Actual shot mix and resulting statistics may change. No continuous server-side ball tracking exists, so the late-quarter exception uses possession context rather than exact travel distance.

# Court Cash V1.2
Basic remains the default 16-pick, 8-player mode. Advanced is 18 picks total, 8–10 players per team, $50 per team, two Gatorades per team per match/series (not replenished between series games; rematches restore post-trade allocation), +3 energy capped at 100 during your own timeout only. Players at full energy or fouled out cannot use a bottle. Trade offers are only before the first game, 3s review buffer then 20s to accept/decline, unlimited declined/expired offers, one accepted trade total, 8–10 players after transfer. Pending trades block Ready and clear both ready votes. Both match formats work in either mode. Existing roster, odds, simulation, and basic auction mechanics unchanged.
